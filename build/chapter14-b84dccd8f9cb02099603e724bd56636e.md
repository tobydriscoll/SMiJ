## Exercises

````{exercise}
:label: exr14-simpsup
To solve the biharmonic equation $u_{xxxx}=f$ with *simply supported* or pinned boundary conditions $u(\pm1)=u_{xx}(\pm1)=0$, we cannot use the same transformation trick as for the clamped case. One way to proceed is to introduce an auxiliary variable $w=u''$ and solve the coupled system

\begin{align*}
u'' &= w, \\ 
w'' &= f, 
\end{align*}

with the boundary conditions $u(\pm1)=w(\pm1)=0$. This allows us to use our usual $\tilde{D}_N^2$ matrix for the second derivative with homogeneous Dirichlet boundary conditions, although the discrete system will be twice as large as before. 

Implement this approach and solve the biharmonic equation with $f(x) = \exp(x)$ on $[-1,1]$ with $N=20$. Plot the solution and its second derivative.
````
